## Welcome

This is the first post on my personal blog. I've been meaning to start writing for a while, and I finally sat down and built this site from scratch using Next.js and Tailwind CSS.

## Why I Started This Blog

Writing forces clarity. When you can explain something in plain language, you understand it. I want to use this space to:

- Document things I learn while building projects
- Share opinions on tools and technologies I use
- Write about problems I've solved (so future-me can find the answer)

## What to Expect

Mostly technical posts — TypeScript, React, systems thinking, and the occasional deep dive into something I found interesting. I'll keep posts focused and practical.

Thanks for reading.
